About KIET

Krishna Institute of Engineering & Technology

Krishna Institute of Engineering & Technology (KIET) is an educational institution focused on providing quality technical and professional education.

The institute aims to develop technically skilled, innovative and responsible professionals who can contribute positively to society and industry.

KIET provides students with academic programs, laboratories, libraries, sports facilities and opportunities for technical and extracurricular activities.
